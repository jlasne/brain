What holds it together
01

A brain remembers the reasons

Each source is read once and becomes a position, with the evidence, the figures and the dates that support it. Ask in plain words and the answer names what it rests on.

02

It argues before it agrees

When a new source contradicts something a brain holds, it says so and asks which one wins. A view you replace keeps its date and stays in the evidence.

03

One subject, one sentence

A brain covers a single subject, and one sentence decides what belongs. That line is why a brain can grow for years and still answer as one mind.

04

Read by any assistant

Every brain here is published. Point your assistant at one address and it answers from this library instead of the open internet.

05

Yours to feed

Only the person who created a brain feeds it, unless they open it to everyone. Feeding runs on your own model key, so the spending stays yours.
