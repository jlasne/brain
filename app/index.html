How it works

One pass per source. The brain is compiled as you feed it, so a question reads positions, never raw transcripts.

Drop

A link, a pasted transcript, a PDF or a Word file. YouTube captions are fetched for you.

Read

Every topic is extracted, with its numbers and quotes. The author is named on every source.

File

Each idea joins a concept or starts one: a position, dated evidence, figures, links to other brains.

Settle

A claim that contradicts a position waits for your ruling. Additions and details go straight in.

Ask

Answers, one-pagers and the map read the compiled positions, with every source cited.

Karpathy's LLM wiki, for people who do not live in a terminal

Andrej Karpathy showed that an AI should compile your sources into a wiki once, rather than search raw files on every question. Octopus takes that idea and adds what a wiki leaves open: who said what, when, and what to do when sources disagree.
