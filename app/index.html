A brain is one subject, with one line deciding what belongs

A scope line is the whole gate. "Health" names a folder. "Sleep, recovery and training load for my own routine" names a brain. That line is what lets a brain grow to any size and stay coherent, because every new idea is measured against it.

How a source lands

  1. Check. The link is normalised and looked up. A source already stored ends here, at no cost.
  2. Read. The whole source is read once, and every topic in it is extracted, whether or not a brain covers it. Then the text is discarded and the link is kept.
  3. Compare. Each idea is measured against the stored position on that concept, which is where repeats and contradictions show up.
  4. Card. One card reports where it goes, what is new, what it repeats, and every claim that clashes. You rule on each clash.
  5. Settle. Each touched position is rewritten from its whole evidence list, your ruling included, and a receipt proves what moved.

Rules that hold on every drop

  • Positions get re-derived, never appended to A position reads as one view. A growing list of who said what is a failed drop.
  • Every view survives, carrying its date A superseded view moves into the evidence. An open conflict stays open, dated on both sides.
  • Each source is checked twice Once by link, once by idea. The second check is what catches a claim you already hold.
  • A summary keeps one line per concept At 9 concepts or 900. Nothing is capped and nothing is trimmed away.
  • Raw transcripts stay outside the brain The link and the location of your own copy are kept. The text itself is discarded after one read.
  • A concept needs three mentions to exist That threshold is what stops a brain filling with one-off ideas.

Asking, at the depth you want

Normal

Three to six lines. Answers straight, numbers inside the prose, sources on one line at the end.

Educational

Defines each term on first use, builds the mechanism in order, and carries one worked example with real numbers.

Expert

Reviews the knowledge base: how many sources stand behind a position, how recent they are, and where the evidence is thin.

Every answer closes with the authors and dates it used. Names stay out of the answer text, because attribution belongs on the sources line.

Read the brains from your own Claude

Octopus publishes an MCP server. Add one address as a custom connector and your Claude reads the positions and their evidence directly, then reasons over them itself. Reading costs nothing, because no model runs on this side.
