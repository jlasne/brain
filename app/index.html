How it works

From a link to an answer you can check, in 4 steps.

Drop it, and it is read.

A link, a PDF, a Word file or a pasted transcript. Video captions are fetched for you.

ArticlePDFVideoWordPodcast

Every claim lands in its place.

Each idea joins a concept or starts one, with its author, its date and its numbers.

Answers you can check.

At 3 levels, or as a one-pager in 9 languages. Each answer names its sources.

A position is re-derived from its whole evidence list at each drop, so a sixth source rewrites the view from all six.
