What makes a brain

"Isn't it just ChatGPT with my files?"

A chat answers from its training. A wiki stacks your files into pages. A brain turns them into positions, each with dated evidence, and asks you when sources clash.

    It argues back

    A source that contradicts what you hold waits for your call. Swipe: keep it, replace it, or both hold. The position is rewritten from all the evidence.

    It keeps score

    Each brain scores out of 10 against your best one: variety of authors, depth, freshness, open conflicts. With the one move that closes the gap.

    It draws itself

    The map: every brain an arm, every concept a sucker, every line a link between brains. A red dot is a clash to settle.
