Connector

Read the brains from your own Claude.

One address, added once. Your Claude then reads the positions and their evidence, and reasons over them itself. No key, no sign-up, and reading costs nothing.

The address

Works on the Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans.

How to add it

  1. Open Claude, then Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Paste the address above. Leave authentication empty.
  4. Ask your question. Claude calls ask and answers from what the brains hold.

The tools it exposes

ToolWhat it returns
askStart here. A question, optionally a brain. Returns the positions that bear on it, their dated evidence, the data, any open conflict, and how to write the answer.
list_brainsEvery readable brain with its scope line and its counts.
read_brainOne brain's scope plus one line per concept.
read_conceptA position, its evidence with authors and dates, its data, its open conflicts.
search_brainsKeyword matches across every concept, title hits ranked first.
list_sourcesWhat a brain has read, newest first, with links.

What a reader gets

✓ Yours to read

  • Every public brain and its scope
  • Positions, evidence, dates, authors
  • Open conflicts on both sides
  • The sources behind a brain, with links

— Held back

  • Private brains, and everything under them
  • Writing: no source lands, no position moves
  • Raw source text, which was never stored
  • Anything past 120 calls per 10 minutes

Why it can stay open

No tool here calls a model, so the endpoint spends nothing and the reader's own subscription does the thinking. That reads better than a cheap model would, and it means an open door costs the brains nothing beyond the database reads, which a rate limit caps.
