The address

How to add it

  1. Open your client's connector or MCP server settings.
  2. Add a remote server over HTTP, sometimes called a custom connector.
  3. Paste the address above. Leave authentication empty.
  4. A client that offers a Connect or Sign in button can skip it. Octopus has no login, because reading needs no key and no account.
  5. Ask your question. The client calls ask and answers from what the brains hold.

Feeding from your own client

The address above reads. To drop sources from your client, sign in to Octopus, open Octopus MCP in the sidebar and make your personal address. It carries your account, so it can feed the brains you own and any brain marked open. The whole setup, step by step.

Feeding spends nothing here. Your client's own model does the reading, the filing and the rewriting. Each tool hands back the exact rules for the next step, and Octopus checks the result before it writes.

The tools it exposes

ToolWhat it returns
askStart here. A question, optionally a brain. Returns the positions that bear on it, their dated evidence, the data, any open conflict, and how to write the answer.
list_brainsEvery readable brain with its scope line and its counts.
read_brainOne brain's scope plus one line per concept.
read_conceptA position, its evidence with authors and dates, its data, its open conflicts.
search_brainsKeyword matches across every concept, title hits ranked first.
list_sourcesWhat a brain has read, newest first, with links.

With a personal address, four more appear. They run in order, and nothing is written until the last one.

ToolWhat it does
drop_sourceYou read the source and send what is worth keeping. Returns the brains, their concepts, and the rules for filing it.
drop_planYou send the filing. Returns the card: where it lands, what it touches, what is new, and every contradiction with the date on both sides.
drop_prepareYou send the person's ruling on each contradiction. Returns the whole evidence list behind each position, and the rewriting rules.
drop_storeYou send the rewritten positions. This writes, and returns the receipt.

What a reader gets

✓ Yours to read

  • Every brain and its scope line
  • Positions, evidence, dates, authors
  • Open conflicts on both sides
  • The sources behind a brain, with links

— Held back

  • Writing, without a personal address
  • Raw source text, which was never stored
  • Anything past 120 calls per 10 minutes
