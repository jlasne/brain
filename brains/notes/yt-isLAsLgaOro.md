## Ideas by topic

### Risk against danger

Finance measures volatility and calls it risk. Volatility is the quantity of movement, and it matters only when you are forced to sell at a low point. For capital held 10 to 20 years, movement is noise.

Danger is separate: the probability the position reaches zero. The two are independent. Sovereign debt of a failing state pays its coupon reliably and goes to zero in one announcement. A salary has zero volatility and ends in one conversation. A chauffeur's income swings hard and carries almost no danger.

Higher volatility correlates with higher potential return. You can lose money on a volatile asset, but you cannot earn a lot on a placid one. Madoff was detectable from that alone: a claimed 10% a year drawn as a straight line contradicts the relationship.

### Value and its displacement

Value is what others want and will pay for. Mowing your own lawn creates none, mowing a neighbour's for money creates it. Entertainment counts, which is why two people paying each other 500 dollars produce real value alongside the GDP.

Innovation moves value rather than shrinking it. The cart destroys the sedan chair, the car destroys the cart. The holder does not decline gradually, the demand leaves. So the investor's first bet, which nobody can place for them, is where value sits in 10 or 20 years.

China held a 4 to 7 century lead in the Middle Ages and missed the industrial revolution. Unlimited labour, working guilds and an intact tax base left no demand for a steam engine. Disruption does not happen in a normal universe.

### Prudence as the trap

Prudence means applying what worked for 40 years to the next 40, so that nobody can accuse you of recklessness. It is a bet that value stays put.

Russian bonds were bought because Russian bonds had worked. That track record was the entire reason and it was worthless.

The three generation pattern follows: the grandfather speculates and risks everything, the son inherits a warning, the grandson is told to preserve. The parable of the talents treats burying the talent as the one unforgivable choice, and Detente reads it as a statement about societies rather than about religion.

Prudence held for most of history because change ran slower than a life. A Roman during the fall experienced no fall, it took 300 years. Progress became perceptible within one lifetime only around the industrial revolution.

### Diffusive systems

A diffusive system takes in energy, fights to keep its structure, and emits heat plus information. Since the big bang these systems have grown denser in energy dissipated per unit mass, on an exponential curve. Human societies now rank above galaxies, stars and particles on that measure.

Macro structures generate micro structures which generate macro structures: galaxies to planets to DNA to species. Density rises as occupied space shrinks, so most of the universe is cold and empty while complexity concentrates. Human geography repeats it, with the Sahara empty and Tokyo dense.

The cycles shorten, which is log-periodicity. That is why prudence stopped working.

### The norm is death

99.9% of species that ever lived are extinct. About 80% of civilisations that existed have gone. Fighting collapse starts from a losing base rate.

Speculating gives 20% odds, matching the civilisational survival rate. Refusing danger out of prudence gives 100% odds of being caught.

### Fragile and antifragile

A fragile asset gains in calm and breaks in disorder. An antifragile asset gains with volatility. Plot excess return against volatility and the slopes run opposite, so the classification is measured rather than asserted.

Gold rises as confidence falls. The yen rises in crisis because Japanese savers repatriate capital, making it antifragile by social structure rather than innovation. Dubai and Swiss property rise with European political volatility, because wealth relocates and families will not move far.

Bitcoin is antifragile in theory, answering a crisis of monetary confidence, with too few observations to confirm it yet.

Holding the wrong one is expensive both ways. An antifragile asset in calm is a vaccine with no epidemic, or paper money invented while gold coins work. A fragile asset in a revolution goes to zero.

Systems reconfigure in short violent episodes. Snow accumulates for months and 30 seconds of avalanche reshapes the slope. Revolutions, wars and monetary collapses occupy very little time and do all the transformation.

### Monetary debasement

The dollar prints about 8% a year. Under 8% return, a dollar portfolio is losing purchasing power while appearing to hold. Nothing marketed as prudent clears 8%.

Spending beyond revenue leaves three options: taxes, which provoke resistance; debt, which taxes the unborn; printing, which erodes existing wealth invisibly. Switzerland runs balanced accounts, France and the United States do not, China runs internal debt.

### Generational cycles

Strong men make peaceful times, peaceful times make weak men, weak men make war, war makes strong men. US policy rates rise to the late 1970s then fall to zero, and the cycle maps onto it. Detente credits Dalio for the long debt cycle framing while noting that a civilisation can fail to recover, unlike a cycle that always turns.

The current generation has no tolerance for volatility, danger or risk, and no longer speculates. Speculation became a slur.

### Energy and compute

The constraint on AI is not total electricity, projected at 3% of world supply by 2030. It is density at one point. A grid behaves like a web: spread the load and it carries, concentrate it and it tears.

A grid is a fixed-cost business, so the load factor sets the price. Idle capacity still has to be amortised, which makes the most expensive kilowatt the one nobody consumes. French nuclear suffers exactly this as deindustrialisation removes demand.

Hyperscalers cannot be flexible. A three-week run across 300,000 synchronised chips cannot pause, so they demand guaranteed supply, which pushes peak capacity up. Gas generation takes 5 years to build and 10 years of permits, against AI timelines of months.

Detente's 2022 bet was that AI would exhaust concentrated capacity and be forced to distribute. Bitcoin mining was the entry, because it starts and stops instantly. Texas farms raised the load factor, lowering prices locally, and were paid to shut off during a polar storm when the megawatt hour went from 40 to 500. They also consume when prices go negative from wind oversupply, which rescues producers.

Data Factory's 2023 fundraising pitched electrical infrastructure rather than mining, despite mining being 100% of revenue. It merged with Hive and Polycloud into Antimatter, which runs container-format distributed data centres on the same slabs. Twenty researchers at Inria convert centralised data centre algorithms for distributed infrastructure.

On centralisation, OpenAI's founding bet under Musk was decentralisation: trade efficiency for resilience, because the unknown arriving is too large to risk one wrong choice. A centralised optimised system is efficient, a decentralised one is resilient. Detente rejects the idea that decentralised maps cleanly onto antifragile.

### Climate

Atmospheric CO2 shows no inflection from any protocol. The timescale is too short for political mechanisms, and acting alone makes it worse for the actor: decarbonise while others grow and you lose economically, the climate degrades anyway, and you end up without the wealth or technology to protect yourself.

Before the industrial revolution, survivors of collapse used rationing and politics. Tokugawa Japan halted deforestation, medieval Icelanders switched from pigs and cattle to sheep and fishing, the Maya mismanaged irrigation and ended.

After it, science became the mechanism. Haber found how to fix atmospheric nitrogen and Bosch industrialised it, removing the Malthusian ceiling for billions. The ozone hole was resolved by substitute refrigerants rather than prohibition, and resorbs by 2045.

The remaining climate lever is extraction, needing roughly the energy that put the carbon up plus losses. The route that could work is CO2 becoming an economic input, wanted for carbon fibre or construction, so the machine captures it for its own reasons.

On Jancovici: read in full since 2010, rated intellectually solid, conclusions rejected because the models assume no technological disruption within the timeframe or at the scale needed.

### Unknown unknowns

There is what you know you can do, what you know you cannot, and what you cannot do without knowing it exists. An entrepreneur lists the risks to the project and fails from somewhere unlisted. A services company founded in 2010 listed employment and margin risks, then met ChatGPT in 2022.

Kurzweil predicted human-level AI around 2030, writing in 2004 by extrapolating curves, and was treated as a crank. Experts extrapolating the past miss systematically.

The conclusion is to hold convictions strongly enough to move fast and change them when conditions change.

### Portfolio construction

Decorrelation is the only way to raise resilience without giving up return. Owning every S&P 500 name looks like sector spread and is one bet, because they fall together.

Three burrows follow from value displacement, antifragility and decorrelation: Bitcoin, high-yield luxury property in countries that articulate between blocs, and compute capacity.

Buying CoreWeave at a price to book of 11 means 11 dollars buys 1 dollar of compute, the rest being expected growth. His answer is selling shares in owned Texas assets at book value.

He borrows from his community at a fixed 8 to 12% depending on size, 100,000 euro minimum, stands as guarantor, absorbs the volatility himself, and validates each investment move with them.
