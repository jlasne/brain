# Decorrelation

## Position

Decorrelation is the only way to raise resilience without giving up return. Everything else trades one for the other.

Holding 500 index names looks like 500 bets across pharma, tech and industry. It is one bet, because they fall together. Real decorrelation means assets that are not chained to the same event.

## Evidence

- 2026, Richard Detente: owning every S&P 500 name gives the appearance of sector spread. When the index cracks, the whole portfolio goes down at once, so the diversification was nominal.
- 2026, Richard Detente: his own answer is three burrows, chosen to be uncorrelated and antifragile: Bitcoin, high-yield property in countries that articulate between blocs, and compute capacity.

## Data

- Three burrows: Bitcoin, property in intermediary countries, compute
- His co-investment structure: fixed 8 to 12% to lenders depending on size, 100,000 euro minimum, with him absorbing the volatility and validating each move with them
