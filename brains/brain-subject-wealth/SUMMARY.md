# Wealth

**Scope:** how the world economy shifts, and where to place my own capital as a result

One line per concept file below. No cap, no trimming.

- Risk and danger: volatility measures movement, danger measures the odds of zero, and they are independent. `01-risk-and-danger.md`
- Value displacement: value moves when innovation lands, and finding where is the one bet nobody can place for you. `02-value-displacement.md`
- Prudence: repeating what worked for 40 years guarantees missing the move when cycles run 10 years. `03-prudence.md`
- Fragility and antifragility: fragile assets gain in calm, antifragile ones gain with volatility, and the split is measurable. `04-fragility-and-antifragility.md`
- Decorrelation: holding 500 index names is one bet, and decorrelation is the only resilience that costs no return. `05-decorrelation.md`
- Diffusive systems: energy density per unit mass rises across the universe in shortening cycles. `06-diffusive-systems.md`
- Monetary debasement: the dollar prints 8% a year, so anything under 8% loses purchasing power. `07-monetary-debasement.md`
- Energy and compute: the grid constraint is density at one point, not total demand, so flexible load is the arbitrage. `08-energy-and-compute.md`
- Climate and techno-solutionism: the CO2 curve shows no inflection from any protocol, so extraction technology is the only lever left. `09-climate-and-techno-solutionism.md`
