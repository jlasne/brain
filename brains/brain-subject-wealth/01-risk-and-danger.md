# Risk and danger

## Position

Volatility and danger are separate axes, and finance measures only the first. Volatility is how much a price moves. Danger is the probability the position reaches zero and stays there. An asset can be calm and lethal, or violent and safe.

For capital held over 10 or 20 years, danger is the axis that matters. Volatility only costs you when you are forced to sell at a low point.

## Evidence

- 2026, Richard Detente: sovereign debt of a badly run state pays the same coupon every year and barely moves, then goes to zero on a Monday morning when the state stops paying. Argentina has defaulted nine times.
- 2026, Richard Detente: a salary carries zero volatility and real danger, because it ends in one conversation. A chauffeur's income swings with the season and carries almost no danger, because Paris will not stop needing rides.
- 2026, Richard Detente: Madoff was detectable from the shape of his curve alone. A claimed 10% a year with near-zero volatility contradicts the relationship between movement and return.

## Data

- Bitcoin daily range cited across 60,000 to 100,000 dollars in the same cycle
- S&P 500 annual volatility 15 to 20%, so a 2% day sits near two standard deviations
- Argentina: nine sovereign defaults
- Madoff: claimed 10% a year at zero volatility
