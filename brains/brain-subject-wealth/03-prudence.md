# Prudence

## Position

Prudence as practised means applying what worked for the last 40 years to the next 40. That is a bet that value stays where it is. When innovation cycles run a decade, the bet loses by construction, and the loss arrives as a step down rather than a drift.

Prudence held for most of history because change was slower than a human life. It stopped holding around the industrial revolution, when progress became visible within one lifetime.

## Evidence

- 2026, Richard Detente: Russian bonds were bought because Russian bonds had worked. Nobody bought North Korean bonds, because those had no track record to extrapolate. The track record was the whole reason, and it was worthless.
- 2026, Richard Detente: the three generation pattern. The grandfather speculates and builds, the son inherits a warning to protect it, the grandson is told to preserve value with no risk taken, which the parable of the talents treats as the one unforgivable choice.
- 2026, Richard Detente: a Roman alive during the fall did not experience a fall. It took roughly 300 years, so at the scale of one life the world had always been as it was.
- 2026, Richard Detente: refusing danger out of prudence gives a 100% chance of being caught. Speculating gives 20%, which matches the share of civilisations that survived.

## Data

- Roman decline: roughly 300 years, longer than any single life
- Civilisations that have disappeared: about 80%
- Species that ever lived and are now extinct: 99.9%
- Odds of survival: 20% by speculating, 0% by refusing
