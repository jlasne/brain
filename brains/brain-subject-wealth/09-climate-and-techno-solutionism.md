# Climate and techno-solutionism

## Position

Atmospheric CO2 has shown no inflection from any protocol or conference. The curve continues on its natural slope, which means political mechanisms have not moved it and the timescale is too short for them to.

Acting alone makes it worse for the actor. A region that decarbonises while others grow loses economically, sees the climate degrade anyway, and ends up without the wealth or the technology to protect itself. Losing twice is the prisoner's dilemma outcome.

So the remaining lever is extraction, which needs roughly the energy that put the carbon up there, plus losses. The route that could work is CO2 becoming an economic input, wanted for carbon fibre or construction materials, so the machine captures it for its own reasons.

The ozone hole is the precedent. It was not solved by banning refrigerants with nothing to replace them. Substitutes were found, and the problem left the conversation.

## Evidence

- 2026, Richard Detente: the CO2 density curve shows no inflection or beginning of one, so claiming climate is a media problem is provocation pointed at the gap between discourse and measured effect.
- 2026, Richard Detente: the ozone hole was resolved by substitute refrigerants rather than by prohibition. It resorbs by 2045 and returns to 1980 levels by 2066.
- 2026, Richard Detente: before the industrial revolution, civilisations that survived collapse did so through rationing and politics. Tokugawa Japan halted deforestation, medieval Icelanders switched from pigs and cattle to sheep and fishing. The Maya mismanaged irrigation and ended.
- 2026, Richard Detente: Haber found how to fix atmospheric nitrogen and Bosch industrialised it, which removed the Malthusian ceiling for billions. That is the industrial revolution's answer replacing the political one.
- 2026, Richard Detente, on Jancovici: he has read all of it since 2010 and rates the work as intellectually solid, while disagreeing with the conclusions, because the models assume no technological disruption within the timeframe or at the scale needed.

## Data

- CO2 atmospheric density: no inflection despite every protocol
- Ozone hole: resorbed by 2045, 1980 levels by 2066
- Serious climate consequences dated around 2050, a 30 year horizon
- Survivors of pre-industrial collapse: about 20%
- Insurers, then reinsurers, are the first to register rising climate losses
