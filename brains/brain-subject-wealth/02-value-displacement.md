# Value displacement

## Position

Value is what other people want and will pay for. It does not stay put. Each innovation moves it, and whatever held it before goes to zero rather than declining gently. The cart maker was not outcompeted on price, the demand left.

So the investor's first job is a bet nobody can place on their behalf: where value sits in 10 or 20 years. Holding today's winner is a bet that the move has already finished.

## Evidence

- 2026, Richard Detente: the sedan chair carrier loses to the cart, the cart maker loses to the car. Each step destroys the prior holder of value rather than shrinking it.
- 2026, Richard Detente: value requires an exchange. Mowing your own lawn creates none, mowing a neighbour's for payment creates it, because a resource changed hands for something wanted.
- 2026, Richard Detente: entertainment counts as value. Two people paying each other 500 dollars produce 1,000 dollars of GDP and real satisfaction, which is why the transaction is not empty.
- 2026, Richard Detente: China held a 4 to 7 century lead over Europe in the Middle Ages and still missed the industrial revolution, because unlimited labour and an intact social order left no demand for a steam engine.

## Data

- China's medieval lead over Europe: 4 to 7 centuries
- 3% of a population is enough to force a change, and 3% of assets enough to move value
